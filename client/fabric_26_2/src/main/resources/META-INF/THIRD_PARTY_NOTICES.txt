AllMusic Client CustomNameplates-style ActionBar background
===========================================================

The compact lyric background is adapted from CustomNameplates' GPL-3.0
`bedrock_2` background assets and adaptive-width layout:

Custom-Nameplates
https://github.com/Xiao-MoMi/Custom-Nameplates
Copyright (C) 2022-2024 XiaoMoMi

Both CustomNameplates and AllMusic are distributed under GNU GPL version 3.
The complete license is included with the AllMusic source distribution.

AllMusic's adaptation generates the equivalent three-slice bitmap at runtime:
14 pixels high, black at alpha 112/255, with one transparent corner pixel.
It does not include or require CustomNameplates' optional text shaders.
